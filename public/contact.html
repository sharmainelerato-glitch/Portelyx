Help & Support

Contact PORTELYX

Need help with PORTELYX, have a privacy question, or want to report something that is not working correctly? This page explains the available support options while PORTELYX is under active development.

Product support

PORTELYX is currently under active development. A dedicated public support email address is being prepared and will be published here when available.

Dedicated support channel coming soon

Privacy requests

Requests relating to personal information, access, correction, deletion or other privacy matters can be submitted through the official PORTELYX contact channel once published.

PORTELYX will not ask you to send passwords, authentication codes or other account credentials as part of a privacy request.

Technical problems

If you experience a technical problem, useful information to include in a future support request may include what you were trying to do, what happened instead, and the device or browser you were using.

Do not include passwords, authentication tokens, banking credentials or unnecessary financial information in a support message.

Financial questions

PORTELYX support can help with questions about how the platform works, but PORTELYX does not provide individual financial, investment, tax, accounting or legal advice.

For advice specific to your personal circumstances, consider speaking with an appropriately qualified professional.

Security concerns

If you believe you have found a security or privacy issue affecting PORTELYX, please avoid publicly posting sensitive details. A dedicated reporting contact will be added as PORTELYX's public support infrastructure is established.

Never share your password. PORTELYX support should never need your password, one-time verification code, authentication token or online banking password to assist you.
