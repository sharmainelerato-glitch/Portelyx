Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

PORTELYX is a Financial Decision Twin that helps people model, simulate, compare and understand possible financial decisions before making them. This Privacy Policy explains the types of information PORTELYX may process, why that information is used, and the choices available to users.

PORTELYX is under active development. Some integrations and account features described below may only apply when you choose to use them and when they are available.

1. Information PORTELYX may process

The information processed by PORTELYX depends on the features you choose to use.

Account information

  • email address;
  • account identifiers used for authentication;
  • account status and authentication information;
  • profile preferences you choose to provide.

Financial information

  • income and recurring expenses;
  • cash, savings and other financial balances you provide;
  • financial goals;
  • transactions or statement information you choose to import;
  • financial information obtained through a supported connection you choose to authorise.

Decision information

  • financial questions and scenarios;
  • purchase or financing options you ask PORTELYX to compare;
  • stress scenarios and assumptions;
  • decisions or preferences you choose to record;
  • information used for decision replay and comparison features.

Technical information

PORTELYX and the infrastructure supporting it may process technical information required to deliver and secure the service, such as request information, error information, device or browser information, and security-related logs.

2. Account authentication

PORTELYX uses Amazon Cognito for user authentication. When you create or access a PORTELYX account, authentication information such as your email address and account identifier may be processed through Amazon Web Services infrastructure.

PORTELYX does not need to receive your account password in its application interface when authentication is handled by the authentication provider.

3. Profile and settings information

PORTELYX may allow you to save preferences such as your display name, country or region, preferred currency, preferred language and application settings.

In the current version of PORTELYX, some profile and application preferences may be stored locally in your browser. Locally stored preferences may not automatically transfer to another browser or device.

4. How PORTELYX uses information

Information may be used to:

  • create and operate your Financial Decision Twin;
  • simulate alternative financial decisions;
  • compare possible financial timelines;
  • stress-test decisions against financial shocks;
  • calculate resilience scores and decision breakpoints;
  • provide decision explanations;
  • support decision history and replay features where enabled;
  • operate account and connected-service functionality;
  • maintain the reliability and security of PORTELYX;
  • diagnose technical problems;
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

5. Artificial intelligence

PORTELYX may use artificial intelligence services to interpret financial questions and explain calculated results in natural language.

Depending on availability and configuration, PORTELYX may use third-party AI service providers to process information required to generate those explanations.

PORTELYX is designed so that deterministic financial calculations are performed by PORTELYX's financial logic rather than being invented by a language model. AI-generated explanations should nevertheless be reviewed carefully and should not be treated as professional financial advice.

6. Financial account and statement connections

If you choose to upload a financial statement or connect a supported financial-data provider, PORTELYX may process information from that source to create or update your financial model.

Connected services may have their own privacy policies and terms. PORTELYX only receives information made available through the connection you choose to authorise and according to the capabilities of the integration.

You should not connect or upload information that you do not have the right to provide.

7. Alexa and connected experiences

If PORTELYX becomes available through a supported Amazon Alexa or Alexa+ experience and you choose to use that integration, information necessary to fulfil your request may be exchanged between Amazon and PORTELYX.

PORTELYX intends to use that information only as necessary to provide the requested connected functionality, maintain account linking, operate the experience and protect the service.

8. Service providers and infrastructure

PORTELYX relies on technology providers to operate parts of the service. Depending on the feature being used, information may be processed through providers supporting:

  • cloud hosting and application infrastructure;
  • authentication;
  • website hosting and delivery;
  • financial-data connections;
  • artificial intelligence functionality;
  • security, reliability and technical operations.

These providers may process information only as required for their respective services and subject to their applicable terms, privacy practices and PORTELYX's configuration of those services.

9. Selling and sharing personal information

PORTELYX does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be disclosed to service providers where necessary to operate a feature, fulfil a request, protect PORTELYX or its users, investigate misuse, or comply with applicable law.

10. Optional analytics

PORTELYX may provide users with controls relating to optional product analytics. Where an optional analytics control is presented as consent-based, PORTELYX will not treat that option as enabled unless the user chooses to enable it.

11. Security

PORTELYX uses reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, misuse or loss.

No internet-connected system can guarantee absolute security. Users should protect their account credentials and report suspected unauthorised account access.

12. Data retention

PORTELYX aims to retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was processed, to maintain the service, protect its security, resolve disputes, or meet applicable legal requirements.

Retention may differ depending on whether information is stored by PORTELYX, locally in your browser, or by a connected service provider.

13. International processing

PORTELYX uses internet-based infrastructure and service providers that may process information in countries other than the country where a user is located. Privacy protections and legal requirements may differ between jurisdictions.

14. Your privacy choices and rights

Depending on where you live and applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information. These may include rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or other information about how your personal information is processed.

You may also choose whether to provide optional information and whether to connect supported external services.

Some information stored locally by PORTELYX can also be removed by clearing the relevant site data in your browser.

15. Account deletion

PORTELYX is developing a complete account-deletion workflow covering authentication and PORTELYX-managed user data. Until that workflow is available, the application will not represent a local-only deletion action as deletion of the complete PORTELYX account.

Users may contact PORTELYX regarding deletion or privacy requests using the contact method made available through the service.

16. Children

PORTELYX is not designed as a financial service for children. Users should only create an account if they are permitted to use the service under the laws and requirements applicable to them.

17. Financial decision-support limitation

PORTELYX provides simulations, calculations and informational decision-support tools. PORTELYX does not provide financial, investment, tax, accounting or legal advice, and simulated outcomes are not guarantees of future results.

18. Changes to this Privacy Policy

PORTELYX may update this Privacy Policy as the service develops, integrations change or legal requirements evolve. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page identifies the latest version.

19. Contact and privacy requests

Questions, privacy requests or concerns regarding PORTELYX may be submitted through the contact information provided through the PORTELYX service or its official application listing.
