Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

PORTELYX is a financial decision simulation platform designed to help users explore, compare, and understand possible financial decisions and their consequences. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when you use PORTELYX and its connected experiences.

1. Information PORTELYX may process

PORTELYX may process information that you provide directly or choose to connect to the service, including:

  • financial profile information such as income, expenses, cash and goals;
  • financial data imported from supported account or statement connections;
  • financial scenarios, decisions and preferences you submit;
  • information required to operate linked voice or conversational experiences;
  • technical information necessary to operate, secure and improve the service.

2. How information is used

Information is used to provide PORTELYX features, including building a financial twin, simulating decision alternatives, stress-testing financial choices, identifying decision breakpoints, generating decision insights, tracking selected decisions and comparing expected outcomes with later observations.

3. Alexa and connected services

When PORTELYX is accessed through a supported Amazon Alexa experience, information necessary to fulfil a user's request may be exchanged between Amazon and PORTELYX. PORTELYX only uses that information to provide the requested functionality and operate the connected experience.

4. Financial information

PORTELYX may process financial information supplied by the user or obtained through integrations the user chooses to connect. This information is used to generate personalised simulations and decision analysis.

PORTELYX is a decision-support and simulation tool. Its outputs are informational and are not financial, investment, tax or legal advice.

5. Data sharing

PORTELYX does not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by service providers or connected platforms where necessary to operate the service, provide a feature requested by the user, maintain security or comply with applicable legal obligations.

6. Data security

PORTELYX uses reasonable technical and organisational safeguards intended to protect information from unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration or loss. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, support its functionality, meet legitimate operational requirements or satisfy applicable legal obligations.

8. Your choices

Users may choose what information they provide to PORTELYX and which supported services they connect. Where applicable, users may also disconnect linked services or request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal information, subject to applicable law.

9. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as PORTELYX develops. The date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was most recently updated.

10. Contact

Questions or privacy requests relating to PORTELYX can be directed to the PORTELYX developer through the contact information provided with the PORTELYX service or its official application listing.
