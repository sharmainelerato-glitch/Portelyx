Important Information

Financial Disclaimer

Last updated: 6 October 2026

PORTELYX helps users explore financial decisions by modelling possible futures, comparing alternatives and stress-testing choices. These tools are intended to support thinking and decision-making, not replace professional financial advice.

Important: PORTELYX simulations are not predictions, guarantees or professional financial recommendations. Real financial outcomes can differ materially from simulated outcomes.

1. Decision-support tool

PORTELYX is a financial decision-support and simulation platform. It allows users to explore questions such as whether a financial decision appears sustainable under a set of assumptions and how different choices may affect a simulated financial position.

PORTELYX does not make financial decisions on behalf of users.

2. Not financial advice

Information provided through PORTELYX does not constitute financial, investment, tax, accounting, credit, insurance or legal advice.

PORTELYX does not act as a financial adviser, investment adviser, broker, lender, accountant, tax practitioner, attorney or other professional adviser.

3. Simulations are based on assumptions

PORTELYX simulations depend on information and assumptions used in a scenario. These may include:

  • income;
  • expenses;
  • cash and savings;
  • purchase prices;
  • financing costs;
  • timing;
  • financial goals;
  • future income changes;
  • emergencies or unexpected costs;
  • other scenario-specific assumptions.

Changes to any of these inputs may materially change the simulated result.

4. No guarantee of future results

PORTELYX cannot know exactly what will happen in the future. Simulations, resilience scores, breakpoint calculations and alternate financial timelines represent modelled outcomes under particular assumptions.

They do not guarantee affordability, savings, investment returns, financial security, credit approval, income stability or any other future result.

5. Decision Resilience Scores

A PORTELYX Decision Resilience Score is an internal decision-support measurement intended to help compare how a simulated choice behaves under the conditions tested by PORTELYX.

A high score does not mean that a decision is objectively safe, suitable or recommended. A low score does not necessarily mean that a decision should never be made.

The score should be interpreted together with the assumptions, breakpoints, stress scenarios and personal circumstances surrounding the decision.

6. Breakpoint analysis

PORTELYX may calculate conditions under which a simulated decision changes from more resilient to less resilient, or vice versa.

These breakpoints are mathematical outputs from the model. They are not promises that a particular price, income, savings level or timing will make a real-world financial decision safe.

7. Financial Chaos Mode and stress testing

PORTELYX may test decisions against hypothetical disruptions such as reduced income, emergency expenses, delayed income or increased costs.

These scenarios are illustrative. They cannot represent every possible event or every consequence that could affect a user's finances.

8. Regret Replay and counterfactual scenarios

PORTELYX may allow users to compare a past decision with a simulated alternative, sometimes referred to as a counterfactual or replay.

These comparisons cannot establish with certainty what would actually have happened had the user made a different decision. They are hypothetical analyses based on the available information and model assumptions.

9. Artificial intelligence

PORTELYX may use artificial intelligence to interpret questions and explain financial calculations in natural language.

AI-generated explanations may contain errors, omissions or misunderstandings. They should not be treated as independent professional advice.

PORTELYX is designed so that deterministic financial calculations are performed by PORTELYX's calculation logic rather than invented by a language model. This design does not guarantee that the overall service will always be error-free.

10. Accuracy of user information

PORTELYX depends on the information available to it. Incomplete, inaccurate or outdated financial information can produce results that do not reflect a user's actual circumstances.

Users should review important financial information before relying on a simulation.

11. Connected financial information

Information obtained through statements or supported financial-data connections may be incomplete, delayed, categorised incorrectly or otherwise differ from information maintained by a financial institution.

Users should verify important balances, transactions, fees, rates and obligations directly with the relevant financial institution when making significant decisions.

12. Investments

If PORTELYX includes features that simulate saving, investing or the possible allocation of surplus funds, those features are simulations only.

PORTELYX does not guarantee investment performance and does not recommend that a user purchase or sell a particular investment, security or financial product.

13. Significant financial decisions

Users should consider obtaining qualified professional advice before making decisions involving substantial financial consequences, investments, borrowing, taxes, legal obligations or other complex financial matters.

PORTELYX should be used as one source of information rather than the sole basis for a significant financial decision.

14. User responsibility

The final decision always belongs to the user. Users remain responsible for evaluating their circumstances, reviewing assumptions and deciding whether to act on information presented through PORTELYX.

15. Changes to this disclaimer

This Financial Disclaimer may be updated as PORTELYX develops, introduces new simulation capabilities or changes how financial information is presented.

The "Last updated" date at the top of this page identifies the latest version.
